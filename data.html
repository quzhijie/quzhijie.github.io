The low-redshift H i master sample

654 low-redshift galaxies and 657 galaxy–QSO sightline pairs with uniform H i Lyα–Lyε measurements from COS, STIS and FUSE, together with SED stellar masses and star-formation rates, adopted distances, halo masses and virial radii, and the per-component Voigt-profile fits.

Accompanies The low-redshift H i cool CGM (Qu et al., in preparation).

  Zenodo DOI and repository links available upon publication.
